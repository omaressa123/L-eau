About the academy

Water is where confidence begins.

L'eau — "water" in French — is the idea behind everything we teach. We are a swimming academy for every age and every level, training in one place: Maadi Club.

Our coaches build swimmers step by step: comfort first, then technique, then endurance and speed. Every swimmer is assessed individually, so nobody is rushed and nobody is held back.

  • Experienced coaches and swimmers
  • Safe, encouraging learning environment
  • Clear 7-level pathway from first splash to competition

The four strokes

Four strokes. One language of water.

Explore the four official competitive strokes. Use the tabs, arrows or your keyboard.

Freestyle

The fastest and most efficient stroke: long, alternating arm pulls with a steady flutter kick and rhythmic side breathing.

  • Arms alternate — one reaches while the other pulls.
  • Flutter kick from the hips, with relaxed ankles.
  • Roll the body and turn the head to breathe.

Backstroke

Swum on the back with a continuous alternating arm motion. The face stays out of the water, which makes breathing natural.

  • Lie flat with the hips near the surface.
  • Arms windmill, entering the water little finger first.
  • Steady flutter kick and eyes looking up.

Butterfly

A powerful stroke where both arms move together and the whole body flows in a dolphin wave, driven by the kick.

  • Both arms pull and recover at the same time.
  • Dolphin movement starts at the chest and flows to the feet.
  • Two kicks per arm cycle, with timing that builds over time.

Breaststroke

A symmetrical stroke: arms sweep and the legs kick in a frog-like motion, followed by a long, streamlined glide.

  • Pull, breathe, kick, glide — in that order.
  • Knees bend, feet turn out, then snap together.
  • Stay long and streamlined during the glide.
